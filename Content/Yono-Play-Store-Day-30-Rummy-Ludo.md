## Is Rummy Ludo one game or two?

The name alone does not tell you. Some apps use two popular game names purely for search visibility and offer mainly one of them; others genuinely include both. Before installing, look at the operator's site and app screenshots and note which modes are actually offered.

If both are offered, compare them as separate products:

- **Rummy mode:** a card game of skill and chance, usually with tables, entry fees and points, pool or deals formats. Our [rummy rules guide](/apk-guides/rummy-rules/) explains how it works.
- **Ludo mode:** a dice-based race game. Online versions often use timed or shortened rules and entry-fee contests.
- **Shared account:** one login and often one wallet, even when the rules differ.
