# Yono VIP Game: APK Source, Login Safety and the SBI Confusion

## Direct answer

Yono VIP is an Android gaming app documented here as its own record. It is **not** YONO SBI, the State Bank of India banking app, and it is not connected to SBI. The download link supplied for this record goes to `uonovipplay.vip`, a domain spelled "uono" rather than "yono". Before installing or logging in, confirm the operator named on that site, the Android package and signer, and the VIP membership and withdrawal terms. Yono Play Store has reviewed this information page; it has not tested the APK, the operator or payments.

## Is Yono VIP the same as SBI YONO?

No. This is the most important fact on this page, because "yono vip" is searched hundreds of thousands of times a month and some of those searches come from people looking for State Bank of India's banking app. If you were looking for SBI banking, close this page and install YONO SBI from the official app store or SBI's own website. Never enter your bank login, card details or banking OTP into a gaming app, and never install a banking app from an APK link in a message. The two are different:

- **YONO SBI** is a banking app published by State Bank of India. It is available on Google Play and the Apple App Store and is used for accounts, payments and loans.
- **Yono VIP** is a gaming app distributed as an APK from a separate website. It has no stated connection with SBI.

## Why is the download domain spelled "uono"?

The supplied destination for this record is `uonovipplay.vip`. The first letter is **u**, not y. We see the same pattern on other records in this catalog: the Yono 777 destination is `uono777network.online` and the Yono Slots destination is `uonoslots.fun`.

The domain does not explain the spelling. It could be a deliberate brand variation, a way around name conflicts, or something else. Our [APK source guide](/apk-guides/how-to-check-apk-source/) shows how to follow redirects and record the final domain. What the spelling does mean for you:

- Typing "yonovip" into a browser will not reach this domain, and a site that does use "yono" may be a different operator.
- A domain name is not proof of who operates a site. Look for the operator's legal name, privacy policy and terms on the final page.
- If a message sends you to a third spelling, treat it as a separate candidate until you can connect it to the same operator.

## What does "VIP" actually mean in Yono VIP?

In gaming apps, "VIP" is usually a marketing word or a loyalty tier, not a status that is verified by anyone. If the app offers VIP levels, the terms should say exactly how a level is reached, what it costs, what each level gives you, and whether any of it can be withdrawn. Read those terms before depositing; our explainer on [credits versus withdrawable balances](/app-safety/credits-vs-withdrawable-balances/) covers the most common misunderstanding. A VIP label does not mean:

- The app is officially approved, licensed or endorsed.
- Deposits are protected the way bank deposits are.
- Rewards, cashback or "VIP bonuses" are guaranteed or withdrawable.
- The app is legal in every Indian state.

## How to log in to Yono VIP safely

Searches for "yono vip login" usually mean someone cannot get into their account. Most account-takeover losses in this category start with a fake support contact rather than a technical hack. Our [login help guide](/apk-guides/yono-game-login-help/) covers common login problems in more detail. Protect yourself with these rules:

- **Log in only inside the app or on the final domain you have already checked.** Do not use login links sent by message.
- **Never share an OTP.** A one-time password is for you to type, not to read out to anyone, including "customer care".
- **Never install a remote-access or screen-sharing app** because someone offers to fix your login.
- **Reset your password through the app's own "forgot password" flow**, not through a contact on Telegram or WhatsApp.
- **Report losses.** If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline **1930** or report at cybercrime.gov.in.

## What should you check in the Yono VIP APK?

An APK file can be renamed freely, so `Yono VIP.apk` tells you nothing about what is inside. Before installing, check:

- **Package name.** It should stay the same across updates. A "new version" that installs as a second app is a different app.
- **Signer.** Android will not install an update signed with a different key over the existing app. If you are told to uninstall first, stop and ask why.
- **Permissions.** A gaming app should not need contacts, SMS, call logs, accessibility services or device-admin rights. Deny anything that does not match a feature you are using.
- **Play Protect.** Keep it switched on. A download page that tells you to disable it is a warning sign.
