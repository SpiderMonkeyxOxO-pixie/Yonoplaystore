## How to test "win" and earning claims before you deposit

Ads and group messages for apps like this often say things such as "win ₹500 daily", "guaranteed earning" or "withdraw instantly". You can test these claims yourself in a few minutes, without depositing. A claim that fails any of the checks below is marketing, not a promise.

- **Find the withdrawal terms.** Look on the operator's own site. If there are none, stop.
- **Check the minimum withdrawal.** A high minimum can make small "daily wins" impossible to cash out.
- **Check which balance winnings go to.** If wins are credited as bonus or coins, they may not be withdrawable at all.
- **Check wagering and expiry.** Bonus-related amounts often have to be played through before withdrawal, and can expire.
- **Check KYC and fees.** Identity checks, taxes and processing fees all apply before money reaches your bank.
- **Look for the word "guaranteed".** No legitimate game of chance guarantees winnings. Treat any guarantee as a red flag.

## Money and account safety

Never pay a fee to "unlock" a withdrawal; that is a common scam pattern. Never share an OTP, UPI PIN or remote access to your phone with anyone claiming to be Jaiho Win support.
