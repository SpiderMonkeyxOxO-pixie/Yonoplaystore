## What an "agent" domain means

In this category, many apps grow through **agents**: people or small businesses who promote an app and earn a commission on the players they bring in. They often run their own websites, Telegram channels and referral codes. A domain like `jaihorummyagent.club` fits that pattern. It does not prove the site is the operator's main website, and the ".club" ending is a general domain anyone can register.

The Download button on this page is also a referral link; see the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/). What this means for you:

- The agent is a middleman. The operator is the company that actually runs the game, holds your account and pays withdrawals. Find the operator's name in the privacy policy and terms.
- An agent's promises, such as "guaranteed bonus", "I'll handle your withdrawal" or "deposit through me for extra", are not the operator's terms.
- If an agent disappears, your only relationship is with the operator. Make sure you know who that is before depositing.


## Safe payment rules for agent-promoted apps

Most losses tied to agents come from payments made outside the app. Follow these rules:

- **Pay only inside the app's own deposit screen.** Never send money directly to an agent's UPI ID, QR code or bank account.
- **Never pay to "unlock" a withdrawal.** Requests for fees, taxes or "verification charges" before release are a common scam.
- **Never share an OTP, UPI PIN or remote access** to your phone with an agent or "support" contact.
- **Keep records:** transaction IDs, screenshots and chat logs.
- **Report losses** to **1930** or cybercrime.gov.in straight away.
