## "Yes" is not consent

The name "Yes Spin" can blur an important line. Tapping "Yes" on a spin, a pop-up or a permission prompt is not the same as agreeing to pay, sharing your contacts, or subscribing to anything. Before each tap, check what it actually does:

- **Does this spin cost money or credit?** The price should be shown before you spin.
- **Does a pop-up start a purchase or auto-renewal?** Look for price and renewal wording.
- **Is a permission request needed for the feature you are using?** If not, choose "Deny".
- **Are marketing messages a separate choice?** You should be able to refuse them and still play.
