## How spins work in apps like Spin 101

Spin apps usually show a wheel or reels, and each spin produces a result. What matters is where each spin comes from, because that decides what you are risking and what you can take out. Before you spend anything, find the terms that answer: which balance does each spin use, which balance do results go to, and which of those balances can be withdrawn? Our explainer on [credits versus withdrawable balances](/app-safety/credits-vs-withdrawable-balances/) and the guide to [spin app reward terms](/app-safety/spin-app-reward-terms/) cover this in detail. Most spin apps mix three kinds of spin:

- **Free spins** cost nothing, but their results are often credited as coins or bonus balance, not cash.
- **Paid spins** use money you deposited. Their results may go to a withdrawable balance, or may not, depending on the terms.
- **Bonus-funded spins** use promotional credit. Winnings from them usually carry wagering requirements and expiry dates.

## Warning signs in spin apps

Spin mechanics make it easy to spend more than you intended, because each spin is quick and results arrive instantly. Stop and step back if you notice:

- Pressure to "spin again" to unlock a prize or reach a threshold.
- A large displayed balance that you cannot find a way to withdraw.
- A fee requested to "release" winnings.
- Support contacts who ask for an OTP, UPI PIN or remote access to your phone.
- No written terms explaining spin prices or reward redemption.
