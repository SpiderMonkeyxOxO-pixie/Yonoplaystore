## What does the supplied domain tell you?

The Download button on this page opens `indrummyvip30.com`. That hostname contains the app name plus two extra parts, **"vip"** and the number **"30"**. Numbered domains are common in this category; they often indicate that an app has moved between several web addresses. ## Should you complete KYC on IND Rummy?

Real-money rummy apps usually ask for KYC (identity verification) before withdrawals, often including a PAN card, Aadhaar details, a selfie or bank account proof. This is sensitive data. Upload identity documents only inside the app or on the checked domain, and stop if KYC is requested through a chat app, a Google Form or a personal email address. Never share an OTP, UPI PIN or your bank login, even during "verification". Before you upload anything, check that the site tells you:

- **Who collects it.** A named legal entity, not only a brand name.
- **Why it is needed and how long it is kept.**
- **Who it is shared with**, for example payment processors.
- **How to delete it** if you close your account.

## What to check in the IND Rummy APK

- **Package and signer.** An update from a new domain should install over your existing app. If you are asked to uninstall first, the new file may be a different app.
- **Permissions.** A rummy app needs network access. It has no clear need for contacts, SMS, call logs, accessibility services or device administration.
- **Play Protect.** Keep it on. Do not follow instructions to disable it.
- **Update source.** Take updates only from the same checked domain, not from links in group chats.
