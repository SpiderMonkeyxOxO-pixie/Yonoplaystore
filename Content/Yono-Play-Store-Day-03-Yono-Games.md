## What safety checks apply across all Yono apps?

Different records require different evidence, but the same stop-or-continue questions are useful throughout the catalog:

- Does the complete app name match the record you opened?
- Does the final domain match the privacy policy and support email?
- Is the operator or developer identified consistently?
- Does every requested Android permission fit a visible feature?
- Are deposits, promotional credits, rewards and withdrawable balances defined separately?
- Is there a written complaint and account-recovery process?

Pause when an app asks for contacts, SMS, accessibility control, continuous location, broad file access or device administration without a necessary feature-based reason. Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, device lock code or remote-control access with someone claiming to provide support.
