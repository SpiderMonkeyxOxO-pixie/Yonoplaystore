## What does "51 bonus" mean in rummy apps?

"51 bonus", "₹51 sign-up bonus" and similar offers are among the most searched rummy phrases. The number changes (41, 50, 51, 75, 100), but the mechanism is usually the same. The app credits your account with **bonus money** when you register or make a first deposit. Bonus money is different from a cash balance:

- **It is usually not withdrawable.** Most apps keep bonus balance separate from your "winnings" or "withdrawable" balance.
- **It is released in small parts.** Many apps let you use only a small percentage of the bonus per game, and only at paid tables.
- **It has wagering or play-through rules.** You may need to play a certain value of games before any bonus-derived amount becomes withdrawable.
- **It expires.** Unused bonus often disappears after a fixed number of days.
- **It depends on KYC.** Withdrawals, including of bonus-derived amounts, typically require identity verification first.
- **It can be removed.** Terms often let the operator cancel bonus balances for rule breaches or multiple accounts.

## How to read a bonus offer before you sign up

The number in an ad is the least important part of the offer. Our explainer on [credits versus withdrawable balances](/app-safety/credits-vs-withdrawable-balances/) walks through a worked example. Before registering, find the bonus terms on the operator's own site and answer these questions. If you cannot find the terms, treat the bonus as worth zero:

- How much of the bonus can be used per game, and at which tables?
- What wagering or play-through is required, and by when?
- Does the bonus expire, and when?
- What must be completed before a withdrawal: KYC, a minimum balance, a first deposit?
- Which fees, taxes or deductions apply to withdrawals?
- Can the operator cancel the bonus, and in what situations?

## How to choose between rummy apps safely

A long list makes it tempting to install several apps at once. Treat each app as a separate company, and decide on the basis of evidence rather than bonus size:

- **Operator identity.** A named company, address, privacy policy and terms on the same domain.
- **Final download domain.** Record it, and use the [APK source guide](/apk-guides/how-to-check-apk-source/) to check it.
- **Package and signer.** Consistent across updates.
- **Permissions.** No contacts, SMS, accessibility or device-admin access for a card game.
- **Clear withdrawal terms.** Minimums, fees, processing time and KYC stated before you deposit.
- **Your limits.** Decide a time and money limit first; see [responsible gaming](/responsible-gaming/).

## Is real-money rummy legal in India?

India's *Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025* prohibits offering and advertising online money games, and several states had their own restrictions before it. Rummy's rules as a card game do not settle the legal status of a paid online table. Yono Play Store lists app records for identification and safety information; it does not recommend real-money play and does not classify any app as legal. Real-money play is for adults aged 18+ only.
